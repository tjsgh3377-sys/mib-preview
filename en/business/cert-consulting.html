Service Overview

We prove the value of technology
through certification.

From New Excellent Technology (NET) and New Excellent Product (NEP) certifications to Innovative Product designation, we support every step — from requirement analysis for each certification to application preparation, presentation, and on-site review response.

NET New Excellent Technology Certification

Eligibility

  • A fully developed technology that has been formalized in theory, built as a prototype, and tested or operated to obtain quantitative performance indicators, and which can be commercialized within 2 years of the application date
  • A fully developed technology that has secured quantitative performance indicators through demonstration testing and can significantly improve the performance of existing products
  • A process technology that can significantly improve the productivity or quality of a product in the future

How to Apply

  • Application rounds — 3 times a year (announced via the Korean Agency for Technology and Standards and the NET website)
  • Required documents — ① New Technology Certification Application ② Technical Description ③ Industrial property rights and certification records from domestic/overseas certification bodies, etc.
  • Review/evaluation fees — 1st review (documents/interview) ₩200,000 · 2nd review (on-site) ₩500,000

NEP New Excellent Product Certification

Eligibility

  • The core technology of the applicant product must be a technology developed for the first time domestically, or an equivalent substitute technology that innovatively improves upon existing technology
  • The performance and quality of the applicant product must be clearly superior compared to similar products
  • A quality management system must be established and operated to ensure consistent production of products of the same quality
  • The product must not infringe on the intellectual property rights of others
  • The technological ripple effect must be significant
  • The economic ripple effect — including export growth and impact on related industries — must be significant

How to Apply

  • Application rounds — 3 times a year (application period and review schedule separately announced via the NEP Mark website, etc.)
  • Required documents — New Product Certification Application, Product Description, Test Report, ISO 9001 Certificate, IATF 16949 Certificate, quality management system documentation, etc.
  • Review/evaluation fees — 1st review (documents/interview) ₩200,000 · 2nd review (on-site) ₩500,000

Innovative Product Certification

The Innovative Product Designation Program identifies products with outstanding public value and innovation to support their entry into the public procurement market. Products designated as Innovative Products gain access to a wide range of public procurement benefits — including pilot purchases by public institutions and negotiated contracts — enabling early market entry and expanded sales channels.

Key Support Services

  • ① Innovative Product Designation Consulting — Reviewing product suitability, analyzing public value and innovation, and establishing strategy
  • ② Private Expert (Scouter) Referral — Identifying promising products, recommending innovative products, and supporting entry into the procurement market
  • ③ Innovative Product Designation Application — Preparing the application, specification documents, and supporting evidence

Benefits of Innovative Product Designation

  • Pilot purchase by public institutions
  • Eligibility for negotiated contracts
  • Entry into the public procurement market
  • Expanded public sales channels

Key Agencies

  • Public Procurement Service
  • Korea Public Procurement Research Institute
  • Innovative Product Support Center
  • Public demand institutions
