Business Overview

Core Components for Semiconductor Front-End Processes
Precision Machining Technology

Semiconductor processes mainly take place in high-temperature, high-pressure plasma environments where deposition and etching occur. Materials such as Si, SiC, and CVD-SiC—known for their excellent corrosion, heat, and chemical resistance—are widely used, and since components for etching processes directly affect yield and process efficiency, securing component reliability and high-precision characteristics is critical. MIB develops and manufactures core components for Etcher and CVD equipment using Micro Hole Drilling, Grinding, Lapping/Polishing, and Cleaning technologies.

Key Products

Material 01

Si (Silicon)

Silicon is the most widely used material in the semiconductor industry, offering excellent electrical properties, high purity, and outstanding machinability. It maintains stable characteristics in plasma process environments and allows precision machining, making it a core component widely applied in semiconductor manufacturing equipment. Based on high-purity silicon, MIB manufactures a variety of semiconductor components that require complex geometries and fine tolerances.

  • High Purity
  • Excellent Machinability
  • High Thermal Conductivity
  • Excellent Plasma Resistance
  • Precision Dimensional Accuracy

Material 02

SiC (Silicon Carbide)

Silicon carbide is a high-performance ceramic material with high hardness, excellent wear resistance, and outstanding heat resistance. It maintains stable physical properties even in high-temperature and plasma environments, and its excellent corrosion resistance and low thermal expansion make it widely used in semiconductor and display process equipment. MIB manufactures core components of various shapes using advanced precision machining technology for SiC materials.

  • Excellent Plasma Resistance
  • High Hardness and Wear Resistance
  • Excellent Heat Resistance
  • Low Thermal Expansion Coefficient
  • Outstanding Corrosion Resistance

Material 03

CVD SiC (Chemical Vapor Deposition SiC)

CVD SiC is an ultra-high-purity silicon carbide material formed through a chemical vapor deposition process, offering a denser microstructure and superior purity compared to conventional sintered SiC. With low particle generation and outstanding corrosion and plasma resistance, it is used as a core material in advanced semiconductor manufacturing processes.

  • Ultra-High Purity Material
  • Exceptional Plasma Resistance
  • Low Particle Generation
  • High Dimensional Stability
  • Outstanding Corrosion Resistance

Material 04

Advanced Ceramics (Advanced Ceramic Materials)

Advanced ceramic materials are core materials widely used in advanced industries such as semiconductors, displays, solar energy, and optics, owing to their excellent mechanical strength, heat resistance, chemical resistance, electrical insulation, and high purity. MIB manufactures precision components optimized to customer requirements based on a variety of high-performance ceramic materials, including Alumina (Al₂O₃), Quartz (SiO₂), and Sapphire (Single Crystal Al₂O₃).

  • Excellent Mechanical Strength
  • Heat and Chemical Resistance
  • Electrical Insulation
  • High Purity
  • Customized Precision Machining

Process

From grinding to cleaning, surface polishing, inspection, and packaging, every process is carried out in-house with our own equipment.

R&D — Corporate R&D Center

LAB 01

Precision Machining Technology for Hard-to-Cut Materials

  • Machining load analysis by wheel type, feed rate, depth of cut, and RPM conditions using a tool dynamometer
  • Derivation of optimal machining conditions for hard-to-cut materials
  • Selection of optimal grinding wheels through machining characteristic analysis by wheel type and condition
  • Machining suitability analysis through surface roughness analysis and optical measurement

LAB 02

Micro-Hole Machining Technology

  • Derivation of optimal drilling conditions based on step feed conditions
  • Analysis of optimal machining depth for chip evacuation
  • Research on cutting and feed speed conditions considering tool rigidity
  • Analysis of micro-drill wear, hole surface roughness, and cross-sections by machining condition

LAB 03

Design and Analysis

  • Securing design stability through thermal deformation structural analysis
  • Securing safety margins by reflecting conditions harsher than actual process conditions
  • Pre-validation of design weak points through stress analysis
  • Securing process stability through plasma gas flow analysis by hole/slot structure
