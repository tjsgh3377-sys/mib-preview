Service Overview

From planning your R&D project
to managing its execution — we're with you.

For science and technology R&D projects in which a central government agency funds all or part of the R&D costs under statutory authority, we support the entire process — from corporate analysis and project matching to business plan drafting, evaluation response, and execution management.

Process

Here's what your company and MIB work on together at each stage.

STEP 1

Planning / Preparation Stage

01Corporate Analysis Stage

Participation eligibility review · Project execution requirements review

Client Company

  • Provide basic company documents
  • (Business registration certificate · list of 4 major insurance subscribers · financial statements for the last 3 years · factory registration certificate · company profile · export records · patents and various certifications)

MIB

  • Analyze project execution requirements
  • Analyze financial/debt/management status
  • Analyze project execution capability
02Technology Analysis Stage

Review technology's suitability for the project

Client Company

  • Discuss development item technology
  • Assign project manager
  • Provide technical data and explain details

MIB

  • Analyze business feasibility
  • Identify core enabling technologies
  • Analyze commercialization potential
03Suitable Project Identification Stage

Analysis and matching of government support programs

Client Company

  • Finalize proposed technology

MIB

  • Analyze program announcements
  • Match proposed technology to suitable projects
  • Review technology trends and item overlap
STEP 2

Application / Evaluation Stage

01Application Submission Stage

Client Company

  • Register on the project management website
  • Respond to requests for supplementary technical data
  • Discuss technical content of the draft plan
  • Finalize project budget and performance indicators
  • Confirm bonus-point items

MIB

  • Advise on the business plan
  • (Development goals · market and commercialization analysis · technology trend analysis)
  • Consult on budget structure and performance indicator target setting
  • Support submission
02Evaluation Stage

Client Company

  • Respond to requests from the managing institution
  • Prepare for the evaluation and presentation schedule
  • Rehearse the presentation with the project manager

MIB

  • Advise on the in-person evaluation presentation
  • Advise on handling the in-person evaluation
  • Advise on anticipated questions and model answers
03Agreement Stage

Client Company

  • Finalize the revised business plan and goals
  • Prepare agreement documents
  • Attend the agreement signing

MIB

  • Advise on the revised business plan
  • Advise on preparing agreement documents
  • Advise on the agreement signing process and procedures
STEP 3

Project Execution Stage

01Project Execution Stage

Client Company

  • Carry out R&D by year/phase
  • Execute budget and keep research notebooks
  • Achieve target performance indicators
  • Provide performance results data

MIB

  • Provide budget execution guidance
  • Provide R&D execution guidance
  • Advise on R&D deliverables
  • Advise on preparing the annual report (by December 31 each year)
  • Advise on preparing the final report (within 2 months of project completion)
02Research Fund Settlement

Client Company

  • Respond to inspections by the dedicated accounting institution
  • Address items noted in ongoing inspections
  • Finalize the research fund settlement

MIB

  • Advise on responding to ongoing research fund inspections
  • Advise on research fund execution during the project period
03Performance Management

Client Company

  • Pay technology royalties
  • Respond to performance surveys

MIB

  • Advise on technology royalty payment
  • Advise on responding to performance surveys
