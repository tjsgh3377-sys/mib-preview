Hello.
Thank you sincerely for visiting the MIB website.
MIB is a specialized company that supports corporate technology innovation and commercialization based on manufacturing technology.

Building on our manufacturing technology and accumulated experience in precision components for semiconductors and advanced ceramics, we respond to the diverse needs of our customers and contribute to strengthening the competitiveness of manufacturing sites.

In a rapidly changing industrial environment, companies are required to possess not only excellent manufacturing technology but also innovation capabilities across various fields such as R&D, smart factories, manufacturing AI, robot automation, and technology commercialization.

In response to these changes, MIB carries out two business areas together: manufacturing and consulting.
In manufacturing, we produce precision components for semiconductors and advanced ceramics based on precision processing technology, and in consulting, drawing on our understanding of and expertise in manufacturing sites, we support the entire process from planning to execution management — covering government-supported R&D, smart factories and manufacturing AI, robot automation, technology certification, and commercialization.

Going forward, MIB will continue to regard customer growth as our highest value, becoming a trusted partner built on our accumulated technology and expertise.
MIB will stand with you as the partner that turns technology into business and connects manufacturing to innovation.

Thank you.

MIB CEO Hwigeun Park
