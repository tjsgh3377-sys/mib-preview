Manufacturing Facilities

Domestic Production Facilities

MIB manufactures core components for the semiconductor and advanced industrial sectors using a diverse range of precision machining equipment, including high-precision MCT, surface grinders, CNC lathes, and cylindrical grinders.

Through machining processes optimized for the characteristics of each material and product, we meet the demands of complex geometries and precise dimensional tolerances, maintaining a systematic production framework that spans cutting, grinding, and precision finishing.

Building on years of accumulated machining expertise and manufacturing experience, we focus on delivering customized products and ensuring consistent, reliable quality.

Vietnam Production Base

MIB is strengthening its global manufacturing competitiveness through its local production base in Vietnam.

Equipped with numerous CNC/MCT precision machining tools and a quality inspection system, we have built a manufacturing infrastructure capable of reliably producing precision components in a wide variety of materials and shapes.

By operating a systematic production and quality management process from machining through to inspection, we respond quickly to our customers' diverse needs and continue to expand a global production system that delivers competitive quality, pricing, and lead times.
