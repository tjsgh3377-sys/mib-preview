History

This is MIB's journey, built on precision manufacturing technology and consulting capabilities together. The full history flows as a single timeline, with color-coded areas for each division.

Manufacturing Consulting Common

  1. 2026

    • Renewed Venture Company certification
    • Certified as a Specialized Materials, Parts & Equipment Company
    • Certified as a Root Industry company
    • Advanced semiconductor-process Si, SiC, CVD SiC, and advanced ceramic component business

  2. 2025

    • Expanded consulting services for NET/NEP innovative product certification
    • Strengthened government-supported technology commercialization and business planning consulting
    • Expanded machining of advanced ceramic materials such as Alumina, Quartz, and Sapphire

  3. 2024

    • Expanded manufacturing AI and smart factory implementation consulting
    • Expanded robot automation implementation consulting
    • Joined the Gumi Semiconductor Industry Association
    • Signed an industry-academic cooperation agreement with Gimcheon University (family company)
    • Expanded manufacturing of Si and SiC precision components for semiconductor processes
    • Secured a technology network for metal component surface treatment

  4. 2023

    • Registered current business site as a business and factory
    • Established an integrated operating system for the Consulting and Manufacturing Divisions
    • Established an in-house R&D center
    • Certified as a Venture Company
    • Designated as a Learning Factory company for Korea Polytechnic University Gumi Campus
    • Designated as a family company of Kumoh National Institute of Technology
    • Established an R&D system for precision machining of hard-to-cut materials and micro-hole machining technology
    • Established an integrated production base for design, machining, and quality control of advanced semiconductor components

  5. 2022

    • Advanced manufacturing innovation and technology commercialization consulting capabilities
    • Expanded planning and execution support for government-funded programs for manufacturing companies
    • Strengthened the precision machining technology base for semiconductor and advanced ceramic components
    • Accumulated expertise in machining condition optimization and surface quality control

  6. 2021

    • Established the foundation for R&D and technology commercialization consulting capabilities
    • Developed precision machining technology for hard-to-cut materials and micro-hole machining
    • Strengthened manufacturing capabilities for molds and metal precision components

  7. 2020

    • Established an integrated R&D-to-commercialization consulting system for manufacturing companies
    • Established a technology base for precision grinding, profile, and outer-diameter machining
    • Entered the manufacturing field of semiconductor and industrial ceramic components

  8. 2019

    • Expanded the technology consulting network and business foundation in the manufacturing industry
    • Built the foundation for precision machining technology and semiconductor component manufacturing business

  9. 2018

    • Secured business planning and consulting capabilities based on manufacturing technology
    • Established a precision machining production base
    • Laid the foundation for MIB's business

Turning technology into business,
connecting manufacturing to innovation.
