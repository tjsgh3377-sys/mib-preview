Manufacturing Division

Precision Parts for Semiconductor Etching Processes

View Manufacturing Division

Silicon

Si

A leading semiconductor material known for excellent electrical properties, high purity, and outstanding machinability. We precision-machine core components such as electrodes, rings, and C-rings.

Silicon Carbide

SiC

A high-performance ceramic material with exceptional hardness, wear resistance, and heat resistance. We use advanced precision machining technology to produce electrodes, plates, and more.

CVD SiC

CVD SiC

A material used in advanced semiconductor processes thanks to its ultra-high purity and low particle generation. We supply high-precision components such as baffles and C-rings.

Advanced Ceramics

Advanced Ceramics

Based on high-performance ceramic materials such as alumina, quartz, and sapphire, we produce precision parts optimized to customer requirements.
