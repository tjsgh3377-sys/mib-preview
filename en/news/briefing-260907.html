Weekly R&D Briefing

This is our guide to government-supported R&D programs as of September 7, 2026. Content originally distributed via our mailing list is also available here on the website.

MIB
MIB R&D BRIEFING
Based on the support program list as of September 7, 2026
MIB Guide to Government-Supported R&D Programs
This Week's Key Highlights
  • Includes the On-Device AI-Based Autonomous Manufacturing Demonstration for Manufacturing and Advanced Robotics/AI-Based Manufacturing Innovation Demonstration programs — companies considering AI/robotics adoption on the shop floor should check these first.
  • Numerous manufacturing-innovation support programs tied to regional industries have been announced, including Future Vehicle Electronic Control Component Upgrades, AI Ship Equipment Demonstration, and Advanced Robotics Process Development.
  • Combines central-government R&D with regional techno-park programs so you can review opportunities by stage: AI autonomous manufacturing → robotics demonstration → core-technology R&D → commercialization.
MIB's Top 8 Recommended Programs
Covers both central-government R&D and regional techno-park/regional innovation agency programs
1. Manufacturing AI / Autonomous Manufacturing
2026 Manufacturing-Specific On-Device AI-Based Autonomous Manufacturing Demonstration Infrastructure Program — Call for Beneficiary Companies (Industrial Innovation Infrastructure Program)
Gyeongsangbuk-do · Korea Institute of Robot and Convergence / Application Deadline: 2026-09-15
Recommended for: Manufacturing companies in Gyeongsangbuk-do pursuing on-device AI, autonomous manufacturing, or intelligent process demonstration
Key Consideration: A demonstration-focused program applying AI directly on the shop floor — recommended first for companies with smart-factory upgrade plans or autonomous-manufacturing projects
View Announcement
2. Advanced Robotics / AI Demonstration
[Jeonnam-Gwangju] SME Manufacturing Innovation Program Using Advanced Robotics and AI — Company Support (Demonstration Support) 2nd Call for Applications
Jeonnam-Gwangju Special Metropolitan City · Jeonnam Technopark / Application Deadline: 2026-09-15
Recommended for: Manufacturing companies pursuing robot/AI-based productivity gains or automation of inspection, logistics, or assembly
Key Consideration: A field-demonstration-focused program suited to companies considering both robot automation and AI adoption at once
View Announcement
3. AI Autonomous ManufacturingClosing Soon
[Chungbuk] Chungju City 2026 AI Pilot Project Demonstration Support Program — Additional Call for Beneficiary Companies (AI Autonomous Manufacturing Leading Project)
Chungcheongbuk-do · Chungbuk Technopark / Application Deadline: 2026-09-10
Recommended for: Manufacturing companies in Chungbuk pursuing AI-based process optimization, quality control, or predictive-maintenance demonstration
Key Consideration: An AI autonomous-manufacturing demonstration program with a near-term deadline — companies with manufacturing data and a concrete improvement target should review it promptly
View Announcement
4. Future Vehicle Manufacturing Innovation
2026 3rd Call for Beneficiary Companies — Future Vehicle Electronic Control Component Industry Upgrade Infrastructure Program
Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy · Gyeongbuk IT Convergence Industry Technology Institute / Application Deadline: 2026-09-14
Recommended for: Manufacturing companies upgrading future-vehicle electronic control components, electrical components, or automotive parts
Key Consideration: A program well suited for Daegu/Gyeongbuk-region auto-parts companies to combine testing, evaluation, and technical support with product upgrades
View Announcement
5. AI Shipbuilding EquipmentClosing Soon
2026 AI Ship Equipment and Advanced Components Demonstration Support Program Announcement
Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy · Ulsan Technopark / Application Deadline: 2026-09-11
Recommended for: Companies with shipbuilding/marine equipment, sensor/control/electrical components, or AI-based ship-component technology
Key Consideration: A regional demonstration program suited for companies preparing to apply and demonstrate AI in shipbuilding/marine components
View Announcement
6. Advanced Robotics Process DevelopmentClosing Soon
[Gyeonggi] Ansan City Advanced Robotics Process Development and Demonstration Program for Manufacturing Innovation
Gyeonggi-do · Korea Institute of Industrial Technology / Application Deadline: 2026-09-11
Recommended for: Manufacturing companies needing robotics process development, automation cell construction, or field demonstration
Key Consideration: Recommended for companies with a concrete robotics-application process that can quantify productivity and labor-substitution effects
View Announcement
7. Robotics R&DClosing Soon
2026 2nd Robot Industry Technology Development Program — Call for New R&D Projects
Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy · Korea Evaluation Institute of Industrial Technology / Application Deadline: 2026-09-11
Recommended for: Companies developing robot components/systems, manufacturing robots, or intelligent robot technology
Key Consideration: Not a demonstration program but a mid-to-long-term R&D project focused on in-house technology development — suited for companies with core robotics technology
View Announcement
8. Multi AI Agent R&D
SME-Manufacturing-Specific Multi AI Agent Development (R&D) Jump-Up Track Implementation Plan Announcement
Ministry of SMEs and Startups · Korea Technology and Information Promotion Agency for SMEs / Application Deadline: 2026-10-30
Recommended for: Companies developing manufacturing-specific AI agents or manufacturing-data-based intelligent decision-making technology
Key Consideration: A new R&D program aimed at AI agent technology development for the SME manufacturing sector — highly worth preparing for over the mid-to-long term by AI solution and manufacturing-software companies
View Announcement
